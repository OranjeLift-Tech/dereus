# Headerachtergronden: generatieprompts

Datum: 18 september 2026. Uitvoering: ingebouwde imagegen, vijf nieuwe achtergrondscènes. Geen stockachtergrond uit de oude Wix-site gebruikt. Geen ongekozen werknemersopties gebruikt.

## Homepage: verhuizing in voorbereiding

Bestand: `home.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website page-header background for a Dutch moving company. Generate a photorealistic landscape photograph, 16:9 wide composition. The photograph will receive a dark royal-blue overlay and centered white heading, so keep the central upper two-thirds quiet and uncluttered with soft natural light and simple low-detail negative space. Main recognizable props near side edges or lower corners, no centered focal object. Natural real textures and subdued blue, wood and neutral tones. No visible people, text, letters, numbers, prices, contact details, slogans, logos, watermarks or fabricated identifiable landmarks. No artificial graphic overlay in the image itself. Scene: a bright understated Dutch terraced-home living room on moving day, a few neatly taped brown moving boxes and rolled moving blankets near the far side walls, a doorway and tall windows softly out of focus in the back. Clear open floor and plain neutral wall through the center, room ready for a careful move. A centered foreground worker cutout will be placed by the website, so keep the middle spacious and free of props.
